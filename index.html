01 / PROFILE

Professional summary

A multidisciplinary professional journey shaped by technology, business, and human development.

I am a technology and business professional with an academic foundation in Computer Science, graduating from the University of the Philippines Los Baños in 2016. I began my career as an Application Development Associate at Accenture Philippines. I then joined BP Integrated Technologies, Inc., where I progressed across software engineering, IT management, and technical leadership. From 2021, I worked as a freelancer in Product Management, managing digital products, web applications, and other initiatives. During this period, I also served as Business Development Director at ReadRight, Inc., contributing to business development and strategic initiatives. Separately, I served as Learning & Development Head for Mental Health & Well-Being at The Learning Place. These experiences broadened my perspective on product strategy, organizational growth, people development, and the importance of well-being in professional environments. In 2026, I took a professional break to pursue postgraduate studies and deepen my expertise. I am currently pursuing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence and Affective Computing through the EIT Digital Master School, with Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE) in Budapest as my 2026 Entry University and the University of Turku in Finland as my planned 2027 Exit University. I expect to complete the program in 2028. My current academic work includes data science, deep neural networks, AI application methods, and innovation and entrepreneurship. I am particularly interested in human-centered AI, affective computing, and the responsible application of intelligent technologies. Looking ahead, I aim to combine my technical background, product and business experience, and understanding of human development to contribute to useful AI products, solutions, and research.
